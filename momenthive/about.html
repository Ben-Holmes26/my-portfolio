About MomentHive

Every guest has a camera. We bring all of it together.

The best photos from your event were never just on the organiser's phone — they're scattered across fifty different cameras. MomentHive puts them all in one hive.

Why we built it

Anyone who has organised a wedding, a party or a reunion knows the routine: you ask guests to send their photos over, and a week later you've got a scattered mess of group chat exports, compressed images, and a few people who forgot entirely.

MomentHive replaces that with one place. Guests upload straight from their phone the moment they take a photo, at full quality, and it lands in a single shared gallery for the event — no app, no account, no chasing.

How it works, in detail

From an empty event to a full gallery

Set up in a couple of minutes

Create an event, give it a name, and MomentHive generates a shareable link and QR code for it. Put the QR code on a table card at a wedding, or just drop the link in your group chat before the party starts.

No accounts for guests

Guests just open the link on any phone or browser and start uploading straight from their camera roll. There's nothing to install and nothing to sign up for, so nobody gets left out because they didn't want to make an account.

Downloads, done with consent

For now, the organiser can download the full-resolution gallery. Sharing every photo with every guest means making sure everyone who uploads is comfortable with that, so while MomentHive is in development we're taking the time to get that consent right. Guest downloads are coming soon, once there's a clear way for everyone to agree to them.

Photos that stick around

Your gallery doesn't disappear the morning after. It stays put for the length of your plan's storage window, so the memories don't get lost in an old group chat that everyone's already muted.
