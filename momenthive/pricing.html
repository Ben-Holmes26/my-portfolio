Questions

Pricing, answered

Do photos expire?

Each plan keeps your gallery for a set window after the event — 14 days on Gathering, 6 months on Event, and for as long as you're subscribed on Host. You can always download everything before it's removed.

Can guests download photos too?

Yes. On the Event and Host plans, you can choose to let every guest download the full gallery for themselves, rather than only the organiser.

What happens after my event ends?

Nothing changes automatically — your gallery stays exactly as it is until the storage window for your plan runs out, so there's no rush to download everything the next morning.

Can I upgrade from Gathering later?

Yes, you can upgrade an existing event to a paid plan at any point if you need more space, more guests, or a longer storage window.

Is there a limit on event size?

The free Gathering plan is capped at 40 photos to keep small events tidy. Event and Host both remove that limit entirely.
